## 🏗️ Team Structure

1. **Core Teams**:

   - **Backend**: Responsible for APIs, services, and database management.
   - **Frontend**: Focuses on user interfaces and client-side logic.
   - **DevOps**: Manages CI/CD, infrastructure, and deployment.

2. **Sub-Teams**:

   - Created for specific features or projects.
   - Examples:
     - `Authentication Sub-Team`
     - `Observability Sub-Team`

3. **Advisory Board**:
   - Senior members who provide guidance and oversee cross-team collaboration.

---

## 🔧 Responsibilities

1. **Team Leads**:

   - Oversee project deliverables.
   - Conduct code reviews and provide feedback.
   - Mentor team members.

2. **Team Members**:

   - Collaborate on tasks and features.
   - Follow coding and contribution guidelines.
   - Participate in team meetings.

3. **Sub-Team Members**:
   - Focus on specific objectives.
   - Provide updates to their parent Core Team.

---

## 🤝 Collaboration

1. **Communication**:

   - Use Discord for real-time discussions.
   - Document decisions and updates in GitHub Discussions or issues.

2. **Team Meetings**:
   - Weekly syncs to discuss progress and blockers.
   - Monthly org-wide updates.

---

## Need Help?

For team-related questions, contact your team lead or use the relevant Discord channel.

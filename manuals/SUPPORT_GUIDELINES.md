## 🛠️ Requesting Support

1. **Use GitHub Discussions**:

   - For questions or feature requests, use the Discussions tab in the relevant repository.

2. **Report Bugs**:

   - Open an issue with the **Bug Report** template.
   - Include detailed steps to reproduce the issue and any relevant logs.

3. **Ask in Discord**:

   - Use the appropriate project or team channel to ask questions.

4. **Escalations**:
   - For critical issues, email `support@itsatot.org`.

---

## 🙌 Providing Support

1. **Be Responsive**:

   - Aim to respond to support requests within **24 hours** during business days.

2. **Document Solutions**:

   - If a question has been answered multiple times, document the solution in the repository's `FAQ.md` or `docs/`.

3. **Collaborate**:
   - Encourage team members to share knowledge and resources.

---

## Support Channels

- **Discord**: Real-time support for team members.
- **GitHub Discussions**: Long-form support and collaboration.
- **Email**: For escalations or sensitive issues: `support@itsatot.org`.

## Need Help?

For any questions about these guidelines, contact the Support Team Lead via Discord or email `support@itsatot.org`.
